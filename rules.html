🎾 Beginners Challenge

Welcome!

This event is designed for Beginner and Upper Beginner padel players who want to gain more match experience in a friendly and supportive environment.

Player Requirements

  • Open to men and women.
  • Suitable for Beginner and Upper Beginner players.
  • Recommended for players with 1–3 months of regular playing or coaching experience.
  • Players should know the basic rules of padel and be able to keep score independently.

Event Rules

  • The tournament lasts 2 hours.
  • We will play 6–7 rounds, with a maximum of 15 minutes per round.
  • Please arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled start time. We will have a short briefing for new players before the tournament starts.
  • Tournament participation must be paid before the tournament starts. Payment can be made through the Racket ID mobile app or directly at the reception.
  • You can cancel your registration free of charge up to 24 hours before the tournament. We kindly ask you to find a replacement whenever possible.
  • Less than 24 hours before the tournament, cancellation is only possible if you provide a replacement player.
  • No-show: If you do not attend the tournament and do not provide a replacement player, you will be responsible for paying the cost of one court.
  • Late to the tournament? You owe the whole crew a round of beers! 🍺😂
  • Fair play, respect for all players, and a positive attitude are expected throughout the event.
  • The goal is to improve your game, gain match experience, and enjoy playing with others.

🎾 Match Rules

  • One whistle = start of the round.
  • Two whistles = end of the round.
  • When the final whistle sounds, finish the point currently being played, then stop the match and record the final score.
  • Each match is played until one team reaches a total of 7 points: 7–0, 6–1, 5–2 or 4–3.
  • If the score is tied when the match reaches the time limit, the final point is decided by Golden Point.
  • Players with a 1000 rating and no match experience/statistics in the Racket ID app will start on the last court in the first round.
