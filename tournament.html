How the evening goes

1

Come 15 minutes early, say hi, warm up.

2

Pairs are drawn at random — new partner each match.

3

Matches are short, so everyone plays with everyone.

4

We count points, then stay for a drink.

What to bring

A racket — or rent one in the club.

Court shoes, water, a towel.

Your share of the court fee.

No experience needed.

FAQ

01

When to arrive?

Please be in advance — 15 minutes before the start.

02

What to do first?

Reach out to us. Usually we will try to make a small brief for the beginners.

03

Where am I playing?

Please check in the app — Racket ID, it will put you on a court.

04

What to do when the tournament begins?

Usually our scheme: 5 minutes warm up, then play for serve — every player has to touch the ball, and then whoever wins the point, that team starts serving.

05

What to do after the match is finished?

Try to fill the points into the app — Racket ID. If you did not manage to do it, reach someone who is leading the tournament and ask them to fill in the results.

06

Do I need a racket?

Bring one if you have it. Usually you can rent a racket in the club.
